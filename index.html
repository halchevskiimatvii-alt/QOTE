    Why Qote

    Quotes shouldn't live in five different places.

    Without Qote

    • Quotes typed in Word and Excel, each one formatted differently
    • Customers scattered across paper, phone contacts, email and chat
    • Sent quotes forgotten, because nothing reminds you to call
    • No clear view of what was accepted and what is still waiting

    With Qote

    • One price catalogue, automatic totals and VAT, a consistent layout
    • One record per customer with every quote and note attached
    • Follow-up reminders tied to each quote
    • A dashboard that shows accepted, pending and expired quotes
    The workflow

    One path from first call to signed-off job.

    Qote follows the order a small business actually works in. Each step feeds the next, so nothing is typed twice.

    1. Request
      A customer asks for a price. Note it down.
    2. Customer
      Create or open their record.
    3. Services
      Pick from your catalogue of services and prices.
    4. Quote
      Quantities, discounts, VAT and validity, totalled for you.
    5. Approval
      Record whether the customer accepted or declined.
    6. Follow-up
      A reminder brings you back to unanswered quotes.
    What's inside

    Everything a quote needs, nothing an enterprise CRM adds.

    Quotations

    Line items, quantities, discounts, taxes and validity dates. Duplicate an old quote for a similar job, follow its status from draft to accepted, and let expired quotes mark themselves.

    Customers

    Notes, statuses, search and a full history of quotes and contacts per customer.

    Price catalogue

    Services, units, default prices and categories. Build a quote in clicks.

    Follow-ups

    Reminders on quotes and customers, so a sent quote never goes quiet.

    Analytics

    Acceptance rate, pending quotes, offered versus accepted value, for any period.

    Import, export, backup

    Bring customers and services in from CSV or Excel. Export or back up your data whenever you like.

    Do I need an internet connection?

    No, not for everyday work. Customers, catalogue, quotes and reminders all work offline. You need a connection for licence checks, updates and sending email.

    Where is my data stored?

    On your own computer, in a local database. Qote Central handles licences, updates and billing, and it holds no customer data.

    What happens if I stop paying?

    You can still open and export everything. Reading and exporting your data is never blocked.

    Can I bring my existing customers and prices?

    Yes. Import customers and services from CSV or Excel, then start quoting from your own price list.

    Can several people use it?

    Yes. Starter includes up to 3 users and Business up to 25, with sign-in, roles and invitations.
